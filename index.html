Souvik Das

About

Souvik Das is an AI Scientist specializing in natural language processing, trustworthy conversational AI, and large language model (LLM) research. With extensive experience across leading institutions such as JPMorgan, Tencent AI Labs, Amazon AGI, and SUNY Buffalo, he has contributed to both industrial and academic advancements in AI. His expertise spans model training, hallucination mitigation, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), and knowledge-grounded dialogue systems. Souvik is passionate about building socially responsible AI systems and has been recognized through multiple publications in top-tier AI conferences.
